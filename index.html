a little about me ✦
About me

Curious about strategy,
obsessed with brands.

I'm Anwesha — still early in my career, but I already know what I enjoy: figuring out the "why" behind a campaign before anyone touches a caption. A few years of agency floors, client briefs, and summit chaos have taught me that the best work starts with an actual plan, not a vibe — and that even the smartest strategy still has to sound like a person wrote it, not a deck.

What I do

Strategy first,
execution that follows through.

01

Campaign Strategy & IMC

Turning a brand goal into an actual plan — I've done this end to end for IGL's social push and Havells' Gen Z repositioning: the brief, the competitor benchmarking, the creative concept, and the one thread that ties it all together.

02

Performance Analytics

Reading the numbers so someone else doesn't have to guess — I've owned the monthly report on a channel that pulled 419.7K views, and traced why one LinkedIn post for the EU hit a 44.91% engagement rate.

03

Client Servicing

The unglamorous but essential bit — I serviced 12+ client accounts at once at RK Swamy, from PSU and government clients to FMCG brands, writing briefs that made sense and staying the calm link between client and craft.

04

Social Media Management

Running a brand's day-to-day online life — I've owned the EU's LinkedIn page, scripted YouTube shorts for a VC firm, and now manage Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook & YouTube for a B2B training venture, calendar to caption.

05

Content & Scripting

Writing reels, founder bytes and captions that sound like an actual person said them — from founder-led shorts for Rukam Capital to the trend-led posts that took Arba Designs from under 100 to 24K+ reach.

06

Live Event & Brand Coverage

On the ground at summits and conferences — I built the entire pre-event content backbone for the Global Inclusive Finance Summit, covered Livelihoods India 2025, and went on-site in Delhi for a government Nukkad Natak activation.
